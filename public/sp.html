📦 Shipping & Policies

🌍 International Shipping: Please allow extra time for delivery (2–8 weeks). Customs and import fees may apply, and delays are beyond our control. For peace of mind, we recommend choosing a tracked option. 💌

⏳ Order Processing

All orders are processed within 1–3 business days. This does not include shipping time. You will receive an email confirmation once your order is shipped.

🚚 Shipping

  • Domestic (USA): • Untracked Mail (Stamps): 5–7 business days. • Tracked Mail (USPS First-Class): 3–5 business days with tracking.
  • International: • Untracked Mail: 2–8 weeks (no replacement if lost). • Tracked Mail (USPS First-Class Intl.): 2–4 weeks, may vary by country.

Note: Once USPS marks a package as delivered, we are not responsible for lost or stolen mail. For peace of mind, please choose tracked shipping.

💳 Refund Policy

Due to the nature of our products (stickers & stationery), we do not accept returns or exchanges. - please check your order very carefully when placing it! Once orders have been shipped, I will not be able to make any changes to your package.

Please contact me if:

  • You listed the wrong delivery address in your order - contact me immediately before your item is posted!
  • Your order is incorrect, damaged, or faulty - send images and your tracking number
  • There are any other issues with your order
  • please reach out within 7 days with photos, and we’ll be happy to help. 💖

You can contact me through this website, DM me, or shoot me an email at 💌 lilthings.club@gmail.com and we'll work something out together! ✪
